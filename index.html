KNOWLEDGE BASE

Frequently Asked Questions

During our initial consultation, our attorneys will carefully review the facts of your situation, assess the applicable laws, and provide an honest evaluation of your legal options and the viability of your case.

Please bring all relevant documents, correspondence, and a written timeline of events. This helps our team understand your situation quickly and provide the most effective guidance from the very start.

We offer transparent fee structures including hourly rates, flat fees, and contingency arrangements depending on the nature of your case. All fee structures are discussed and agreed upon before we begin any work.

Case timelines vary based on complexity, the type of case, the jurisdiction, and the willingness of parties to settle. Most cases settle within 12-18 months, though complex trial cases may take several years.

Mediation can be a faster, less expensive, and more private alternative to court. Our attorneys will advise you on the best approach based on the specifics of your case and desired outcome.
